# Contributing

Thank you for your interest in A-share Company Intelligence.

## What is welcome

- **Bug reports.** Open an issue describing what you ran (the desktop action or the `ashare-cli` command), what you expected and what happened, with the version (`ashare-cli version`) and the relevant lines of the log.
- **Changes at the sources.** CNINFO, the exchanges and the quote list change without notice. A report that an address or a response format has changed, with the self-test report if possible, is especially useful.
- **Questions and suggestions** about the method, the codebook or the documentation.

Please do not include personal information, login details or large extracts of disclosure documents in an issue.

## Pull requests

The software is licensed under the PolyForm Strict License 1.0.0 (`LICENSE.md`), which does not permit changes to the software or its distribution. Pull requests are therefore not accepted at present. Please describe the change you have in mind in an issue instead; the author may implement it. Suggestions made in issues may be used in the project without any obligation to the person who made them.

## Security problems

Do not report a security problem in a public issue. See `SECURITY.md`.
